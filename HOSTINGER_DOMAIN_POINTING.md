# Hostinger Domain Pointing to Vercel

## ✅ What You Did

You provided Hostinger with:
- **Your Vercel URL:** `https://gnanova-ai.vercel.app/`
- **Your Domain:** `gnanova.pro`

Hostinger will configure the domain to point to your Vercel site.

---

## 🔍 How Hostinger Will Do This

Hostinger has two ways to connect your domain:

### Option 1: DNS Records (Recommended - Best Performance)
- Hostinger adds DNS records pointing to Vercel
- Domain directly connects to Vercel
- Full functionality, fast, proper SSL

### Option 2: URL Forwarding/Redirect
- Hostinger redirects `gnanova.pro` → `gnanova-ai.vercel.app`
- Shows Vercel URL in browser (or masks it)
- Simpler but less ideal

---

## ✅ What to Expect

### If DNS is Configured Properly:
- ✅ `https://gnanova.pro` → Shows your Vercel site
- ✅ SSL certificate works (HTTPS)
- ✅ Fast loading
- ✅ Professional (no redirects)

### If Using URL Forwarding:
- ⚠️ May show Vercel URL in address bar
- ⚠️ Might have redirect delays
- ⚠️ Less professional appearance

---

## 🎯 Best Practice: Configure DNS in Vercel Too

For the best setup, you should also add the domain in Vercel:

### Step 1: Add Domain in Vercel
1. Go to Vercel Dashboard
2. Select your project: `gnanova-ai`
3. Go to **Settings → Domains**
4. Click **"Add Domain"**
5. Enter: `gnanova.pro`
6. Click **"Add"**

### Step 2: Vercel Will Show DNS Records
- Vercel will display the exact DNS records needed
- Share these with Hostinger support
- Or add them yourself in Hostinger DNS settings

### Step 3: Hostinger Updates DNS
- Hostinger adds the DNS records Vercel provided
- Domain will point directly to Vercel
- SSL certificate automatically configured

---

## ⏱️ Timeline

- **DNS Changes:** 5-30 minutes (can take up to 24 hours)
- **SSL Certificate:** Automatic, usually within minutes
- **Domain Active:** Once DNS propagates

---

## 🔍 How to Verify It's Working

### Test 1: Visit Your Domain
- Go to `https://gnanova.pro`
- Should show your Vercel site
- Should have HTTPS (lock icon)

### Test 2: Check DNS Propagation
- Visit [whatsmydns.net](https://www.whatsmydns.net)
- Enter `gnanova.pro`
- Check if it points to Vercel

### Test 3: Check in Vercel
- Go to Vercel → Settings → Domains
- Should show `gnanova.pro` as "Valid Configuration"
