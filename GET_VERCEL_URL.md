# How to Get Your Vercel Website URL

## 🌐 Find Your Live Website URL

Your site is deployed! Here's how to get the URL:

### Method 1: From Deployments Page

1. **Click on the deployment** (the one showing "Ready" and "Production Current")
2. You'll see a **URL** at the top, something like:
   - `https://gnanova-ai.vercel.app`
   - Or `https://gnanova-ai-[random].vercel.app`

### Method 2: From Project Overview

1. **Click "Overview"** tab (top navigation)
2. Look for **"Domains"** section
3. You'll see your deployment URL there

### Method 3: From Settings

1. **Click "Settings"** tab
2. Go to **"Domains"** section
3. Your default Vercel URL will be listed

---

## 🔗 Your Website URL Format

Your URL will be something like:
- `https://gnanova-ai.vercel.app`
- Or `https://gnanova-ai-[your-username].vercel.app`

---

## ✅ Next Steps

1. **Visit your URL** - Click on it to see your live website!
2. **Test it** - Make sure everything works
3. **Share with Hostinger** - Give them this URL
4. **Add Custom Domain** (optional) - You can add your own domain later
